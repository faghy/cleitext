# Share Buttons

The official Cleitext plugin for share buttons, each with its icon:

- **Social networks:** Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, and on request Bluesky, Threads, Reddit, Pinterest, Tumblr, Messenger, LINE, Viber, VK and XING.
- **Fediverse:** Disbigle, Diaspora (it asks for the pod), Mastodon and “Others”, through [Share₂Fedi](https://s2f.kytta.dev), which asks for the server and covers Mastodon, Misskey, Friendica, GoToSocial and more.
- **Email** and **Copy link**.

Instagram has no address to share a link: its button copies the link and opens Instagram, where it can be pasted.

It is not in the base package: download `cleitext-plugin-share-buttons-<version>.zip` from the Releases page and upload it in **Plugins → Upload Plugin**, or install it from the catalog.

## Settings

**Settings → Share Buttons**:

- the social networks and the fediverse networks, in the order shown (add, remove and drag them);
- email and copy link;
- the look: icons and names, or icons only;
- where they go by themselves: after the content of posts, before, both, or only where the **Share buttons** block is placed; optionally on pages too;
- hide the basic share links of the site (Settings → Discussion), so that they are not shown twice; the like button stays.

The **Share buttons** block puts them anywhere, also in Fresco and in the block editor.

## Privacy

No tracking and nothing loaded from other sites: the buttons are plain links to each service's share page, opened only when the visitor clicks. A small script completes the links with the page's full address and copies the link.
